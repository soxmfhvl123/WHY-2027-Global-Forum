Design Leadership

ABOUT


HYUN-SUN KIM is a pioneering leader in Korean public and urban design, currently serving as Chair of the Korean Federation of Design Associations (KFDA). She has advanced the field through integrated approaches that connect individual experiences to the broader urban landscape, setting new standards in urban color planning, environmental design, and public policy. As Chair of the Asia Design Prize Jury and a key advisor to both the Seoul Metropolitan Government and national policy committees, she has played a central role in shaping Korea’s design ecosystem.

Guided by a philosophy of empathetic and human-centered design, Chair Kim has strengthened the social and economic impact of designers in contemporary society. Under her leadership, KFDA unites 25 member associations and partners with regional institutions to drive policy innovation, provide public-sector consulting, and foster industry-academia exchange,

ensuring Korean design continues to lead and inspire on a global stage.

• Led the international jury, evaluating and awarding outstanding design projects across Asia

• Provided strategic direction and governance for Seoul’s leading design foundation

• Supervised major public design projects and fostered collaboration with international partners


Korea Landscape Council – Vice President (2013-2020), Advisor (2023-Present)

• Led initiatives to empower women designers and expand professional networks
