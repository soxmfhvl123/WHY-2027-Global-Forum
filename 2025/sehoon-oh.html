Design Leadership

ABOUT


SE-HOON OH is a visionary leader who has transformed Seoul into a global hub and a pioneer of sustainable urban solutions, while also expanding its local stage into the world’s foremost platform for design, knowledge discourse, and sustainable practice. He places at the heart of his approach the fundamental question, “Why must we build this city together?”, valuing the very journey of seeking answers alongside each citizen.

Since spearheading the planning and completion of Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) in 2006, he rebranded Seoul as Asia’s Design Capital. In 2021, he was elected Vice-Chair of the C40 Cities Climate Leadership Group, where he has shared and scaled climate and environmental innovation strategies across more than 100 member cities. Through the “Oh Se-hoon Law,” he exported Seoul’s model of transparent governance to international municipalities, and as a KOICA advisor and endowed professor, he has led the cultivation of global talent and cross-cultural collaboration. His “local-to-global” mindset—anchored in strategic insight, multicultural empathy, and field-driven execution—proposes a new paradigm of urban governance centered on uncovering and sharing meaningful insights.
