The Design Mindset

ABOUT


ENRIQUE MARTINEZ GONZÁLEZ is a globally recognized expert in design, innovation, strategy, and leadership development.

With over 30 years of experience across architecture, industrial design, and civic leadership, he has pioneered a humancentered approach to innovation that transforms not only products and systems, but also the very mindsets of those who lead them. As the founder and director of Muchiest LLC, Martinez has led programs that empower government executives to navigate complexity through creative problem-solving, strategic foresight, and insight-driven leadership. Rooted in both practice and pedagogy, his work is deeply informed by over 18 years of teaching at RISD and by academic appointments at Brown University, Harvard Law School, and the American University of Sharjah. Across all contexts, Martinez remains committed to designing systems that are both socially impactful and creatively empowering. He reminds us that meaningful change doesn’t begin with answers — but with better questions, and with leaders bold enough to design the future rather than wait for it. His lifelong dedication to reframing challenges through empathy, collaboration, and iterative inquiry demonstrates the potential of a design mindset to foster human-centered innovation across diverse disciplines and systems.
