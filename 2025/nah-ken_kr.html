The Design Mindset

ABOUT


KEN NAH is a designer and practitioner whose philosophy is grounded in empathy and a deep understanding of humans’ contexts and emotions. He carefully crafts user experiences and emotional flows, and realizes them as safe, dignified environments through rigorous ergonomic validation. By combining engineering thinking with creative insight, he develops actionable innovations that scale into organizational, urban, and policy-level strategies, yielding tangible impact. Alongside teaching at IDAS, Hongik University, and Dongseo University, he has served as Chief Designer for the City of Busan, General Director of the 2023 Gwangju Design Biennale, General Director of WDC Seoul, and a board member of the World Design Organization (WDO), working to advance design’s social impact and sustainable value. Through a wide range of activities—public-policy advising, exhibition curation, and regional collaboration—he stresses that design goes beyond problem-solving to generate deep insights and drive long-term change and sustained value.
