The Design Mindset

ABOUT


SANG-YUP LEE is a visionary automotive designer whose work transcends mere styling to craft vehicles that connect deeply with the human spirit. Convinced that a car functions as an emotional conduit, he unravels the fundamental question of purpose by seamlessly interweaving heritage and innovation across four design pillars—styling, architecture, proportion, and technology—to forge cohesive narratives that resonate with everyday life. Refined through pivotal roles at Porsche, Pininfarina, General Motors, Volkswagen’s California Design Studio, and Bentley, Lee rose to lead the Hyundai & Genesis Global Design Center. As Executive Vice President, he now imbues concept icons like Prophecy and 45 Concept—as well as production models such as the IONIQ 5 and GV series—with a timeless presence and forward-looking ethos, while guiding a diverse, creative team. His leadership not only defines brand identity but also inspires designers to honor legacy and embark on bold explorations of the future, prompting a fresh appraisal of design’s intrinsic purpose and value.
